# Agent Loops

Created: 2026-04-12T13:33:39.448Z

Agent loops are a fundamental concept in the design of autonomous systems. They describe the cyclic process through which an agent (an entity that can perceive and act upon its environment to achieve goals) senses its environment, processes information, makes decisions, and acts upon those decisions. This cycle allows agents to adapt and respond dynamically to changes in their environment.

**Note:** Agent loops often involve feedback mechanisms where the outcomes of actions are used to refine future decisions, improving the agent's performance over time.
