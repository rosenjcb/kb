# LLM Providers

Created: 2026-04-12T13:32:41.406Z
Tags: AI, Technology, LLM

LLM (Large Language Models) providers are companies or organizations that develop and offer large-scale language models for various applications, including natural language processing, text generation, and language understanding. Some of the prominent LLM providers include OpenAI (known for GPT models), Google (known for BERT and other models), and Facebook AI (known for their contributions to natural language processing technology).
