\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduced KB, a local-first, facts-first codebase knowledge system.
KB's deterministic AST indexing and multi-pond BFS retrieval provide
agents with structured, persistent codebase knowledge without per-session re-exploration.
A post-retrieval fact curator enforces relevance on the scored pool before
\texttt{kb query} uses one-shot synthesis to produce plain prose answers.
Init-time embeddings (local by default) feed semantic scoring during retrieval.
Paired harvest evaluations
(\ResultsUpdated{}) report $\Delta S = \KbSelfCheckDeltaS{}$ on the \texttt{kb} self-check
suite and $\Delta S = \RaylibDeltaS{}$ on raylib, with K-side scores on seven expanded
suites (\texttt{nifi}, \texttt{shellcheck}, \texttt{lazygit}, \texttt{duckdb},
\texttt{mitmproxy}, \texttt{fish-shell}, \texttt{brew}; Table~\ref{tab:harvest-results}).
\ResultsControlStatus{}.

We also introduced MOEL, a multi-objective evaluation framework spanning two settings:
(1)~paired harvest evaluation, whose headline grade is $\Delta S = S_K - S_N$ from
runs comparing \texttt{kb query} to a real coding agent on the same suite questions;
and (2)~deep MOEL evaluation ($L_\text{MOEL}$), which measures exploration loss under
toy-tool N/K/O conditions per task.  Bias-mitigated jury ensemble, statistical calibration,
and programmatic validators distinguish efficient exploration from expensive correctness.
Future work includes completing paired control harvests for the expanded suite portfolio and
tracking whether $\Delta S$ gains persist as KB indexing and curation mature.
