# KB Philosophy 

We've reached a new era of computing with LLMs and agentic flows...

## Guiding Principles

### Descriptive, not Prescriptive

The AI community has pumped out standards like [BMAD](https://github.com/bmad-code-org/BMAD-METHOD), [SpecKit](https://github.com/github/spec-kit), and [Get-Shit-Done](https://github.com/gsd-build/get-shit-done) but they all suffer from the same issue: they're overly prescriptive and rigid system that burn through your token budget. In truth, they're just Waterfall in disguise. 

`kb` aims to avoid all of this and maintain the [Agile Manifesto](https://agilemanifesto.org)
```
Individuals and interactions over processes and tools
Working software over comprehensive documentation
Customer collaboration over contract negotiation
Responding to change over following a plan
````

Truth is that while making a plan is good, it's often not....

In other words, `kb` is not a top down tool that intends to disrupt your workflow. It's a silent observer that dutifuly keeps record - [like the court historians of the Joseon era](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veritable_Records_of_the_Joseon_Dynasty).


### Knowledge bases as a source of truth

Knowledge bases exist because there exists a need for a source of truth for business logic, infrastructure, architecture, etc.
